💡 ACTION 1 BIN RATIO

Reallocate Bin Ratios to General Waste

General Waste bins reach 100% capacity by 11:30 AM daily while Recycle bins remain <30% full. JMB is shifting 2 bins to General Waste to completely prevent floor overflow.

🐱 ACTION 2 PET HYGIENE

Dedicated Airtight Pet Litter Station

18% of severe odor spikes stem from heavy soiled cat litter bags dumped in open bins. JMB is installing a specialized sealed Ground Floor Pet Waste Chute with bio-enzymatic odor liners.

⏱️ ACTION 3 SLA SHIFT

Adjust Contractor Emptying Schedule

Shift cleaner collection window from 3:00 PM to 12:00 PM to intercept the lunch disposal surge, reducing floor dumping by an estimated 78% without increasing labor costs.
